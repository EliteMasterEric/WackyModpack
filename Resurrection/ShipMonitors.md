# To Use
QuotaInfo: Total days, quota number, and deadline on one monitor.
ProfitQuota: The current profit and quota progress.
Storage: Contents of self-sorting storage.
Sales: What items are on sale today.

NetWorth: The current credit wallet plus the value of scrap in the ship.
DailyProfitOrSoldScrap: The value of scrap dropped in the ship today.
ScrapLeft: Amount of scrap items outside the ship, and their value.
AverageDailyScrap: The average of the amount of scrap collected each (work) day.

PlayersAlive: The number of players currently alive.
FancyWeather: The current weather, but FANCY ASCII
TotalDeaths: The total number of deaths we have had.
CurrentMoon: The name of the current moon.

PlayerHealth: Cycles showing the health of players.
ExternalCam: The view outside the ship.

# To Ignore
TotalQuotas: The current quota number we are on.
Deadline: The number of days until the quota is over.
OvertimeCalculator: Try to calculate the amount of overtime received.
SoldScrap: The value of scrap sold to the company today.


CompanyBuyRate: The current company buy rate, up to 100%
CustomText: Fully customized text.
DangerLevel: The danger level of the moon.
DaysSinceDeath: Days since a player died.
DoorPower: The % power the pneumatic door has.
PlayerHealthExact: Cycles showing the health of players.
Time: Current time of day.
Weather: The current weather.
